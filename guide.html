Sijill — the teacher's guide

Qur’an School · five minutes to read, once. Keep it on your phone.

Before your first class

  1. Open sijill-app.pages.dev
  2. Type the school passphrase. Once on this phone — it remembers you.
  3. Pick your name. If it isn't there, tap My name is not on the list and add it; you can teach straight away and a coordinator confirms it later.
  4. Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.
    iPhone → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android → ⋮ → Add to home screen.

No account and no password beyond the school one. Everything you record is signed with your name, so a mistake can always be traced back and corrected — by you.

Know this before you record anything

Every family has a read-only page of their own child, reached by a private link. What you mark, and any note you write, is what they see. Not instantly — but by the time they next open it.

Write what you would be comfortable reading aloud to that child's mother.

Friday and Sunday — the register

Mark all present first, then fix the exceptions. Most children are there most weeks, so that is one tap instead of thirty.

✓here here · late · away Attendance. Away opens the reasons — pick one.
✓heard ✓ heard They recited everything they had open and it was all fine. One tap and that child is done. It only appears when the child is marked present and has homework open.
Jad H. › The name Their page, for anything that isn't the simple case.

The bar at the top says how many children are still incomplete and stays on screen while you scroll. A child counts as complete with both an attendance mark and a recitation. Green means you are finished.

The arrow in front of each name

That child's direction. There is a key underneath the list in the app too.

moving forwardNew ground, or review recited well.
standing stillPresent, but nothing passed.
going backwardsUnprepared, absent without reason, or stuck repeating.
not enough to sayAway, or nothing recorded yet. This is what you will see in the first week, and it is correct.

The child's page

The three marks

✓good goodRecited to standard. Three mistakes a page or fewer.
↻again againMore than three mistakes. It comes back next week by itself — you do not have to set it.
–not ready not readyHere, but hadn't worked on it. A conversation with the family, not a teaching problem, and it deliberately changes nothing.

Getting through the class

Next student › at the bottom walks you through without going back to the list. If the next name isn't the next child you'll hear, tap 1 of 8 ⌄ at the top and pick whoever you want.

Setting homework

+ Assign new memorization or + Add old memorization to review. Search by name or by surah number. Leave the ayah boxes empty for the whole surah.

If you set something the child already has open, the app stops and shows you — you can still assign it again if that is the point.

To fix one you got wrong, tap the ✎ on the homework line. You can change the surah, the ayat, whether it is new or review, and the note. Change the surah and leave the ayat empty and it becomes the whole of the new surah.

Notes

At the bottom of the page. You can read, change and delete them. Parents see notes.

Recording what a child already knows — the audit

This is the part that replaces the paper sheet, and it is worth ten minutes of your attention.

Tap ☰ Mushaf on any child's page. You get the whole Qur'an as thirty juz, each labelled with where it starts and ends — Al-Faatiha 1 → Al-Baqara 141 — and how many of its pages that child has solid.

Tap a juz and you get, in this order:

  1. The whole juz, with its own three marks.
  2. The surahs in that juz, each with its number and its own three marks.
  3. Hizb by hizb, page by page. Tap a page open to mark just part of a surah.

At every level:

✓solid solidThey have it.
↻review reviewThey had it and it needs work. Use this — not clear — when a child has forgotten something: it keeps the history and puts the passage back into the review rotation.
–clear clearRemove it from the record. For correcting a mistake you just made, not for recording that a child forgot. It asks first.

When to use it

  • A child joins mid-year. Sit with them, work down juz 30, mark what they have. A whole juz is one tap — do not tap 37 surahs.
  • You discover they know more than the record says. Mark it and move on.
  • The September re-audit. This is how it is done now, instead of on paper.

What the audit does not do

It does not record that the child recited today. It records what they already have, and leaves their place in the review rotation untouched — which is deliberate. A child whose state you entered from last year's paper has not been heard this week, and pretending otherwise would hide them from the rotation for a month.

Use the three marks on the child's page for what happened in class today. Use the mushaf for what was already true.

The children's names — please leave them short

You will notice the register says Jad H., not Jad El Haiba. That is deliberate, and it is the one thing in here I would ask you not to "fix".

Every child is stored as their first name and the initial of their surname. Nothing else. No date of birth, no address, no school, no photograph. Their parents' names, phone numbers and email addresses are not in the system at all — there is nowhere in the database to put them. We find the adult by looking the child up in ClassDojo, which the school already uses.

This matters because the app lives on the internet. A child's full name plus their mosque plus their schedule is enough to identify a family; a first name and an initial is not.

Names can be corrected, and sometimes need to be. The import got compound first names wrong — "Abu Bakr Sangirov" came out as "Abu S." when it should read "Abu Bakr S.". If a child's name is wrong, tell Tarek. When you see it corrected it should still be first name plus one initial. Please don't type a full surname in, even to be helpful.

If a name is so short it is ambiguous — two children called Anas in one class — tell Tarek. There are better answers than a full surname.

Older students can have their own link

A parent link covers one adult and the children they are responsible for. But a student old enough to have their own phone can have their own link, separate from their parents' — the same page, showing only them, and without the attendance warning, which is a message for adults.

Ask Tarek if one of your older students wants one.

Two things that will happen

You tapped the wrong thing

Everything is reversible.

  • Attendance, or a ✓ heard → ↶ Undo last change at the top of the register.
  • A recitation already marked → the child's page → Recent classes → ↶ Take back. Their memorisation returns to what it was and the homework opens again.

The line stays in the record marked as withdrawn, because when two people remember an evening differently, the fact that someone corrected themselves is exactly what you want to be able to see.

It won't save

A red banner appears with Retry. Nothing is lost while it is showing.

If retrying keeps failing: write the marks on paper and tell Tarek. Do not spend the class fighting a phone instead of teaching.

What you cannot do, on purpose

  • Close a class day (snow, Eid, no teacher). Coordinator only — message Tarek. On a closed day every child is automatically excused with the reason, so it counts against nobody. You can still set homework for next week; only recitation is blocked, because none happened.
  • See parents' names, numbers or emails. They are not in the system at all.
  • See another family's link. Several teachers here are also parents.

Settings

The ⚙ top right: العربية switches the whole app to Arabic and back, light or dark, coordinator tools, and sign out.

Everything saves as you tap. There is no Save button anywhere, and closing the app loses nothing.
