# Sijill · سِجِلّ — the teacher's guide

Ommah Madrasah. Five minutes to read, once. Keep it on your phone.

---

## Before your first class

1. Open the link Tarek sent: **sijill-app.pages.dev**
2. Type the **school passphrase**. Once on this phone — it remembers you.
3. Pick **your name**. If it isn't there, tap *My name is not on the list* and
   add it; you can teach straight away and a coordinator confirms it later.
4. **Add it to your home screen** so it opens like an app:
   iPhone → Share → *Add to Home Screen*. Android → ⋮ → *Add to home screen*.

No account, no password beyond the school one. Everything you record is signed
with your name, so a mistake can always be traced back and corrected — by you.

---

## Friday and Sunday: the register

**Mark all present** first, then fix the exceptions. Most children are there
most weeks, so that is one tap instead of thirty.

Each row then gives you:

| Tap | What it does |
|---|---|
| **✓ here · L late · ✗ away** | Attendance. *Away* opens the reasons — pick one. |
| **✓ heard** | *They recited everything they had open and it was all fine.* One tap and that child is done. |
| **the name ›** | Their page, for anything that isn't the simple case. |

**✓ heard** only appears when the child is marked present *and* has homework
open — no homework means there is nothing to hear.

The bar at the top says **how many children are still incomplete** and stays on
screen while you scroll. A child counts as complete with **both** an attendance
mark **and** a recitation. Green means you are finished.

The small **arrow in front of each name** is that child's direction. There is a
key underneath the list:

| | |
|---|---|
| ↗ green | **moving forward** — new ground or review recited well |
| → amber | **standing still** — present, but nothing passed |
| ↘ red | **going backwards** — unprepared, absent without reason, or stuck repeating |
| — grey | **not enough to say** — away, or nothing recorded yet |

## The child's page

### The three marks

- **✓ good** — recited to standard. Three mistakes a page or fewer.
- **↻ again** — more than three mistakes. It comes back next week **by itself**;
  you do not have to set it.
- **– not ready** — here, but hadn't worked on it. This is a conversation with
  the family, not a teaching problem, and it deliberately changes nothing.

### Getting through the class

**Next student ›** at the bottom walks you through without going back to the
list. If the next name isn't the next child you'll hear, tap **1 of 8 ⌄** at the
top and pick whoever you want.

### Setting homework

**+ Assign new memorization** or **+ Add old memorization to review**. Search by
name *or by surah number*. Leave the ayah boxes empty for the whole surah.

If you set something the child already has open, the app stops and shows you —
you can still assign it again if that is the point.

To fix one you got wrong, tap the **✎** on the homework line. You can change the
surah, the ayat, whether it is new or review, and the note. Change the surah and
leave the ayat empty and it becomes the whole of the new surah.

### Notes

At the bottom of the page. You can read, change and delete them. **Parents see
notes** — write what you would be comfortable reading aloud to that child's
mother.

## Recording what a child already knows — the audit

This is the part that replaces the paper sheet, and it is worth ten minutes of
your attention.

Tap **☰ Mushaf** on any child's page. You get the whole Qur'an as thirty juz,
each labelled with where it starts and ends — *Al-Faatiha 1 → Al-Baqara 141* —
and how many of its pages that child has solid.

Tap a juz and you get, in this order:

1. **The whole juz**, with its own three marks.
2. **The surahs in that juz**, each with its number and its own three marks.
3. **Hizb by hizb, page by page**. Tap a page open to mark just part of a surah.

At every level the three marks mean:

- **✓ solid** — they have it.
- **↻ review** — they had it and it needs work. Use this rather than *clear*
  when a child has forgotten something: it keeps the history and puts the
  passage back into the review rotation.
- **– clear** — remove it from the record. This is for correcting a mistake you
  just made, not for recording that a child forgot. It asks before it does it.

### When to use it

- **A child joins mid-year.** Sit with them, work down juz 30, mark what they
  have. A whole juz is one tap; do not tap 37 surahs.
- **You discover they know more than the record says.** Mark it and move on.
- **The September re-audit.** This is how it is done now, instead of on paper.

### What it does not do

**It does not record that the child recited today.** It records what they
already have. Their place in the review rotation is untouched, which is
deliberate — a child whose state you entered from last year's paper has not
been heard this week, and pretending otherwise would hide them from the
rotation for a month.

Use the three marks on the child's page for what happened in class today. Use
the mushaf for what was already true.

## Two things that will happen

**You tapped the wrong thing.** Everything is reversible.

- Attendance, or a ✓ heard → **↶ Undo last change** at the top of the register.
- A recitation already marked → the child's page → **Recent classes** →
  **↶ Take back**. Their memorisation returns to what it was and the homework
  opens again. The line stays in the record marked as withdrawn, because when
  two people remember an evening differently, the fact that someone corrected
  themselves is exactly what you want to be able to see.

**It won't save.** A red banner appears with **Retry**. Nothing is lost while it
is showing. If retrying keeps failing: **write the marks on paper and tell
Tarek.** Do not spend the class fighting a phone instead of teaching.

---

## What you cannot do, on purpose

- **Close a class day** (snow, Eid, no teacher). Coordinator only — message
  Tarek. On a closed day every child is automatically excused with the reason,
  so it counts against nobody. You can still set homework for next week; only
  recitation is blocked, because none happened.
- **See parents' names, numbers or emails.** They are not in the system at all.
  Families are reached through ClassDojo, as before.
- **See another family's link.** Several teachers here are also parents.

---

## Settings

The **⚙** top right: **العربية** switches the whole app to Arabic and back,
light or dark, coordinator tools, and sign out.

Everything saves as you tap. There is no Save button anywhere and closing the
app loses nothing.
